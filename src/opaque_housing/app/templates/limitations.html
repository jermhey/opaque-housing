Limitations

  • LLC membership-interest sales generate no deed. Flow undercounts entity turnover.
  • Co-op buildings are corporate-owned by design and are never counted as opaque.
  • Trusts are not entities. Most are probate-avoidance vehicles.
  • Philadelphia unit weights are official building-code lower bounds, not a dwelling census.
  • Cook County and Miami-Dade are county files, not Chicago or City of Miami. Harmonized sfr_1_4 includes 2–4 unit buildings that are not NYC 1–4 family.
  • Cook owner names are intermittent; land trusts and TAXPAYER OF hide beneficial owners.
  • The free Florida SDF has no buyer name, so Miami-Dade entity-buyer shares are not identified.
  • Name-key HHI is not a portfolio map. Shared officers can falsely merge clusters; citywide top portfolios stay unpublished.
  • This app does not search addresses, BBLs, OPA accounts, or owner names.
  • GitHub-hosted refresh cannot cold-pull ACRIS, NY DOS, PHL RTT, or Miami-Dade. Cook County stock and sales are on the monthly job.
  • Gold labels for individuals stay out of the public repo and off the public VM.
