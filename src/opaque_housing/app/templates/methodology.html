Methodology

The pipeline classifies the recorded owner name on each residential tax lot, then publishes citywide and neighborhood aggregates. This app only reads those files.

Owner classes

One class per owner record. First matching rule wins. Entity-owned = llc + corp + partnership. Trusts are a sensitivity series. Public and nonprofit owners leave the private denominator. Co-op buildings are identified by official building class, not by name.

Mix-adjusted compare

From stock_by_building_type.csv in both metros. Philadelphia rates are applied to New York’s building-type mix (weights renormalized on types both metros have). Kitagawa decomposition uses Philadelphia as the reference mix: NYC − PHL = rate effect + mix effect. NYC co-ops have no Philadelphia analogue and are excluded from the standardized rates.

Definition playground

Stock slices come from the four headline series already in headlines.json (all private vs 1–4+condo; entity-only vs entity+trust; lot vs unit). Flow slices come from flow_sensitivity.csv ($0 / $10k / $100k, PHL exclude-sheriff). Nothing is reclassified at request time.

Anonymous concentration

HHI and top-N shares are computed on the laptop from classified parcels, keyed by owner_key (or opacity cluster_id when present). Published files contain neighborhood totals only. Citywide ranked portfolios are not published: opacity cluster 1 is a known likely false merge (officer-name chain).

Correction

NYC headlines may include Rogan–Gladen on the held-out gold test split. Philadelphia has no gold correction. Claim cards show raw and corrected when both exist.

Full rules, ADRs, and filter counts live in the repository under docs/.
