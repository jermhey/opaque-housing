Entity share by building type

Unit-weighted share of private residential lots whose recorded owner is an LLC, corporation, or partnership. Co-op buildings are a separate class and are not treated as opaque.

Opacity tiers among entity-owned units

O1 = a natural person is named on that owner’s HPD or DOS record. O2 = no person on this owner, but a cluster parent is O1. O4 = linked records point only to other entities. O3 = residual no-person. Trusts have no tier.
