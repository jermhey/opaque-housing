Limitations

  • LLC membership-interest sales generate no deed. Flow undercounts entity turnover.
  • Co-op buildings are corporate-owned by design. They are never counted as opaque.
  • Trusts are not entities. Most NYC living trusts are probate-avoidance vehicles. They appear only as a sensitivity series.
  • PLUTO has no owner mailing address and stores condos as billing lots, not unit owners.
  • Staten Island sales are almost missing from the DEED-based headline. Those ACRIS rows are mostly RPTT.
  • No live PAD file. Condo unit-lot ↔ billing-lot mapping is fixture-tested only.
  • Historical stock is not a full-city panel. It is lots with a named sale deed in 2003–2025, attributed with current PLUTO building type and units. 2025 coverage is about 62% of current private lots.
  • NY DOS is active entities only. Dissolved corporations are missing (survivorship bias).
  • O1 is a person on that owner’s filing, not a beneficial owner. Registered agents are denied. Cluster 1 (590 owners) is a likely false merge on shared officer names.
  • Gold correction uses a stratified name sample, not a simple random sample of lots. The same name-level matrix is applied to unit-weighted shares.
  • GitHub-hosted refresh cannot cold-pull ACRIS or NY DOS (ADR 0009). Monthly Actions may update PLUTO stock and reuse the last flow and opacity series.
  • Philadelphia has no dwelling-unit census on OPA. Unit-weighted PHL shares use official description lower bounds (ADR 0010). Compare parcel shares.
  • PA DOS publishes no officer names on 3urc-uaba. Philadelphia has no O-tiers in this release.
  • No individual names or addresses are published. There are no entity or portfolio pages.
Honest limits are also in the repository README.
